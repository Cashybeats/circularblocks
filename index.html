THE DIRECTION

Where precision
meets possibility.

Circularblocks is being developed around four connected interests. This portfolio will document the work as it takes shape.

01

CNC

Exploring geometry, toolpaths and the relationship between a digital drawing and a physical part.

02

Manufacturing

Considering materials, repeatable processes and the details that make an idea practical.

03

Crafting

Making room for hands-on experiments, finishes and learning through the material itself.

04

Design

Connecting purpose, proportion and construction through sketches and considered decisions.
